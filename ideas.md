**Design Movement:** Dark Technical Minimalism meets Academic Precision
**Core Principles:**
1. Deep dark backgrounds with high-contrast data-visualization-inspired accents
2. Monospaced code-like typography for labels, paired with a clean serif for body text
3. Geometric grid overlays and circuit-board-inspired decorative elements
4. Dense information hierarchy with clear visual lanes

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#0d1117) background evoking a terminal/IDE, with electric cyan (#00e5ff) as the primary accent — communicating precision and intelligence. Warm amber (#f59e0b) used sparingly for highlights. The palette signals "serious researcher who ships real products."

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric sidebar-anchored layout. A thin fixed left rail carries section labels and a vertical progress indicator. Main content flows in a wide right column with occasional full-bleed breakout sections for publications and the interactive demo.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Animated particle/node graph in the hero — echoing social graph trajectory prediction work
2. Scanline or grid-dot texture overlaid on section backgrounds at low opacity
3. Monospaced section counters (01 / 02 / 03) as visual anchors

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states reveal hidden data layers (e.g., citation counts, paper abstracts). Scroll-triggered entrance animations feel like data loading into a dashboard.

**Animation:** Staggered fade-up on section entry; hero particle graph animates continuously; publication cards flip on hover to reveal abstract.

**Typography System:** `Space Grotesk` (bold display) + `IBM Plex Mono` (labels/code) + `Inter` (body). Hierarchy: 72px hero → 36px section → 20px body.

## Selected Design: Dark Technical Minimalism (Response 1)

**Rationale:** This design best reflects Dr. Mohamed's identity as a researcher who bridges academia and industry. The dark terminal aesthetic resonates with his deep technical work in ML systems and edge AI, while the particle/node graph hero directly references his trajectory prediction research. The asymmetric layout with a left rail creates a sophisticated, non-generic structure that stands out from typical portfolio templates.
